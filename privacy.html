Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Benhong Denim Signal handles information when providing its internal denim trend research service.

1. About the application

Benhong Denim Signal is an internal trend research tool for a denim garment manufacturer. It helps merchandising and product-development staff review aggregated fashion and search-interest signals.

2. Information we access

The application may access aggregated Pinterest Trends information, including trending keywords, relative search-interest data and growth rates. This information is used only for internal trend research.

The application does not request or collect Pinterest passwords, private messages, private boards, private Pins, payment information or sensitive personal information.

3. How information is used

Aggregated trend information is used to:

  • identify growing denim-related search keywords;
  • compare trend signals across Germany, France and Italy;
  • support internal product and merchandising research;
  • improve the accuracy and usability of the trend dashboard.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information or Pinterest data. We do not share accessed data with unrelated third parties. Trend results are intended for authorized internal business users.

5. Data storage and security

API credentials and access tokens are stored securely on the server and are not exposed in public website code. We retain only the information reasonably necessary to operate and improve the internal service.

6. Third-party services

The application may use Pinterest APIs and publicly available trend sources. Use of Pinterest services is also subject to Pinterest's own terms and privacy policies.

7. User choices and requests

Users may contact us to ask questions about this policy or request the deletion of information associated with their use of the application.
